About Tinkl

Tinkl is an English learning platform designed for practical, everyday improvement. It combines speaking practice, sentence correction, vocabulary building, English tests, and buddy conversations in one place.

You can practise real-life English, check your spoken sentences, learn new words, test your knowledge, and communicate with other learners through text, voice, or video features.

Privacy

Tinkl is designed to provide learning features while keeping account and practice information within the services used by the platform. Please review the platform's current privacy information before using account or communication features.

Terms

Use Tinkl respectfully and only for lawful English-learning communication. Do not share harmful, abusive, sexual, or illegal content. Communication features may be moderated or restricted to protect learners.
