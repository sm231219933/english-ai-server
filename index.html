About Tinkl

Tinkl is an English Learning Platform built to help learners improve practical English through speaking, vocabulary, tests and real conversations.

🎤 Speaking Practice

Speak everyday sentences and practise natural English.

✏️ Sentence Correction

Speak a sentence and get grammar feedback.

📚 Vocabulary

Build useful English vocabulary with examples.

🤝 Learn With Buddy

Practise English through text, voice and video conversations.
